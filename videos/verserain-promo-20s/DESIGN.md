# VerseRain Promo Design

## Style Prompt

A bright, polished mobile-game promo for VerseRain: scripture memory as refreshing rain, clean white cards, crisp blue UI, garden growth, friendly competition, and a hopeful Christian learning tone. The motion should feel light, readable, and app-store ready rather than cinematic or dark.

## Colors

- Rain blue: `#3b82f6` for primary actions and brand marks.
- Deep scripture navy: `#172033` for main text.
- Garden green: `#10b981` for growth and rewards.
- Team pink: `#ec4899` for competition moments.
- Challenge orange: `#f97316` for energy and calls to action.
- Cloud white: `#ffffff` with soft blue shadows.
- Mist background: `#eef4fb`.

## Typography

- Display and brand headlines: `Outfit`, `system-ui`.
- Verse and supporting copy: `Outfit`, `system-ui`.
- Use strong weights, generous line height, and no negative letter spacing.

## Motion Rules

- Rain and cards glide in with soft ease-out movement.
- Scene changes use clean crossfades or wipes, never hard cuts.
- Keep every text beat readable for at least 1.5 seconds.
- Use subtle scale and glow for emphasis, not shaky or chaotic motion.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use dark, heavy, apocalyptic rain.
- Do not use generic purple gradients or stock-tech styling.
- Do not overcrowd the frame with many small UI details.
- Do not let text touch device safe areas or crop at the edges.
