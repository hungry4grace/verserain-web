# 雲端家人 — Daily Reach & Connection (Design Doc)

**Author:** Drafted for David · **Date:** 2026-06-21 · **Status:** Proposal for review

> Goal: use VerseRain's 雲端家人 (Cloud Family / Companion Teams) to reach more people
> *daily*, in the chat apps they already use, and turn a tap into a real signal of
> connection — without breaking the feature's "gentle, no-pressure" soul.

---

## 1. The idea, in one line

Each day, a warm, good-looking link goes out to the group chat (LINE / WeChat / WhatsApp).
One tap opens **today's verse**, credits the reader, and lets them respond in one more tap —
which shows up in the family feed as connection.

David's instinct ("a shared link sent through social media daily to nudge and connect")
is the right one. This doc explains why, what already exists, the one risk to manage,
and a three-layer build plan.

---

## 2. What 雲端家人 already is

Companion Teams: admin-led small groups for Bible reading, deliberately designed
**against competition**. Four red lines (from `TEAMS.md`):

1. No ranking. 2. No "you're behind" framing. 3. Milestones, not score deltas.
4. Admins are shepherds, not referees.

Members mark verses complete, write reflections/prayers, and cheer one another
(emoji + short notes). There's a non-ranked "fruit garden" point system and an
admin-only **Gentle Nudge** panel that quietly flags members who haven't opened the
schedule or have gone silent 7+ days.

## 3. What already exists (most of the plumbing is built)

This is the key finding — David's idea is closer to shipping than it looks.

| Capability | Status | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Per-day verse deep-link** — opens straight to today's reading/challenge and **credits "today done"** without a manual join | ✅ Built | `buildVerseShareUrl()` → `verserain.com/?startSet=…&teamId=…&verseIndex=…&mode=campaign` (`src/teams/TeamsModal.jsx`) |
| **Native share hand-off** to LINE/WeChat/etc. | ✅ Built | `navigator.share()` in the schedule item's Share button (`TeamsModal.jsx` ~L1083) |
| **Join deep-link + QR** | ✅ Built | `buildJoinUrl()` → `verserain.com/?join=CODE` |
| **Web push: store/list/send** | ✅ Built | `/save-push-subscription`, `GET /push-subscriptions`, `api/push-team-cheer.js` |
| **Daily-verse image generation** | ✅ Built | `scripts/generate-dailyverse-backgrounds.mjs` (`npm run generate:dailyverse-backgrounds`) |
| **Scheduled daily trigger** (cron/alarm) | ❌ Missing | — push only fires today on a *cheer*, never on a schedule |
| **Social link-preview cards** (OG/Twitter meta) | ❌ Missing | `index.html` has only a generic `<meta name="description">` |
| **Frictionless "I read this" reply on the landing** | ⚠️ Partial | credit is given, but no one-tap reaction back to the feed from the link |

**Takeaway:** the daily share link is essentially done. The two real gaps are
(a) an automated daily trigger and (b) link previews that look good in a group chat.

## 4. My honest assessment

**Why the approach is right.** Meeting people inside the group chat they already live in
beats relying on push notifications, which most people mute. A link that opens straight to
today's verse *and* counts as read is low-friction and gives a measurable engagement signal.

**The one risk to manage — tone.** A daily automated blast cuts against the product's whole
"gentle, no pressure, shepherds not referees" ethos. In this audience, anything that feels like
spam or a guilt-trip will backfire. So every surface must stay **invitational**: a warm daily
card that opens to read in one tap, plus an easy way to respond — never "you haven't read yet."

**One technical reality for the "social media" part.** VerseRain is a single-page app with **no
Open Graph / Twitter meta tags**. Paste a link into LINE/WeChat today and the preview is generic
and unappealing. For something meant to grab attention in a group chat, the preview card is not a
nice-to-have — it's most of the battle. (This is Layer 2.)

## 5. Build plan — three layers

Ship in order. Each layer stands alone and de-risks the next.

### Layer 1 — "Today's Family Card" (quick win, mostly client-side)

An admin one-tap button: **「今日家人卡 / Today's Family Card」**. It auto-selects today's
scheduled item and generates a nicely formatted share card —

```
📖 Psalm 37 — Inherit the Land · Day 3 / 7
Ps 37:4
「Delight yourself in the Lord, and he will give you the desires of your heart.」

👉 https://www.verserain.com/?startSet=ps37&teamId=…&verseIndex=2&mode=campaign
```

— then fires `navigator.share()` so it forwards to LINE/WeChat in one tap.

- **Effort:** small. ~90% exists; mainly auto-pick today's item by date + a nicer card format.
- **Reuses:** `buildVerseShareUrl()`, existing share button, schedule `targetDate`.
- **Validates:** do people actually forward and tap? (Ship this first, measure, then invest.)

### Layer 2 — Social-ready link previews (the attention piece)

A small Vercel serverless function returns HTML with real OG/Twitter meta tags for the verse/daily
links: `og:title` = passage, `og:description` = verse text, `og:image` = a daily-verse card.

- **Effort:** medium. New function under `/api`, plus a rewrite so share URLs hit it for bots/crawlers
  while humans still get the SPA.
- **Reuses:** `scripts/generate-dailyverse-backgrounds.mjs` for the preview image.
- **Why it matters:** turns a bland gray link into an eye-catching verse card in the group chat —
  this is what makes the daily link spread.

### Layer 3 — Automated daily nudge + feedback loop (the recurring engine)

1. **Scheduled send.** A daily job (PartyKit `alarm()` or a Vercel cron) that, per team each morning,
   sends today's verse to members **who opted in** — reusing `GET /push-subscriptions` +
   `api/push-team-cheer.js`. Per-team send time; opt-in respected.
2. **Close the loop.** When someone taps the link, the landing offers a one-tap **「🙏 已讀 / Amen」**
   + optional one-line reflection that posts to the team feed. The admin then sees *who engaged*
   as connection — e.g. "今天 12/20 家人已讀 / 12 of 20 read today" — kept **gentle and unranked**,
   consistent with the Gentle Nudge design.

- **Effort:** larger. New scheduling mechanism + per-team settings + a lightweight landing reaction
  endpoint.
- **Guardrail:** opt-in only, one nudge/day max, warm copy, no "behind" language.

## 6. Recommended sequence

1. **Layer 1** — ship, then watch forward/tap rates for ~1–2 weeks.
2. **Layer 2** — once people are sharing, make the link look good so it spreads.
3. **Layer 3** — automate the daily send and the feedback loop last.

## 7. Open questions for David

- **Which chat apps matter most?** (LINE? WeChat? WhatsApp?) — affects card format and preview testing.
- **Who sends the daily link** — the admin manually (Layer 1) or fully automated (Layer 3)?
- **Send time** — one global time, or per-team admin-chosen?
- **Opt-in default** — should auto-daily push be off by default (recommended, matches the gentle ethos)?
- **Feedback depth** — is one-tap "已讀/Amen" enough, or do you want a short reflection prompt too?
